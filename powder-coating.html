Powder Coating

What You'll Pay

Powder coating is a dry finish. A fine powder is sprayed onto the part, which holds an electrostatic charge, and the part is then cured in an oven so the powder flows into a hard, even coat that is tougher and more chip resistant than most wet paints.

We do it the bespoke way. Our powder coating is fully manual and offline, so every part is hung, sprayed and checked by hand rather than sent down a conveyor line. That is what makes the difference on odd shapes, one-offs and anything where the finish really matters.

It is also a cleaner process than wet spraying. Powder coating gives off no harmful solvents or airborne pollutants, and our booth has a waterfall filtration system that draws the air through a curtain of moving water to catch loose powder.

We price every powder coating job individually. Size, the number of parts, how much preparation is needed and the colour and finish you choose all affect the cost. Send us a photo with rough sizes and the colour you have in mind, or call us, and we will give you a price before we start.
The Process

What to Expect

  1. Pick your colourTell us the finish you are after. If you already have a RAL code or a sample, send it with your enquiry.
  2. PreparationParts are shot blasted back to clean metal first. Good adhesion comes from good preparation, and this is the step most cheap coating skips.
  3. Masking and coating by handThreads, bores and contact faces are masked off, then the powder is applied by hand in our offline booth.
  4. Curing and checkingThe part is cured in the oven, then checked by eye before it leaves the unit.

Every coated part starts with shot blasting. For parts that need a very thin, heat resistant coat instead, take a look at Cerakote.
