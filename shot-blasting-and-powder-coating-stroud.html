Shot Blasting and Powder Coating in Stroud

Stroud

Local Shot Blasting and Powder Coating You Can Trust

Stroud's Five Valleys have been making things for centuries, and many of the old mills now house workshops, fabricators and small manufacturers. We take on shot blasting, powder coating and specialist finishing for Stroud customers, from contract parts for local businesses to gates, railings and garden furniture for the cottages and farmhouses that line the valleys.

Plenty of the metalwork around Stroud is old and one of a kind, and that is exactly the work that suits a hand-finished process. Nothing goes down a conveyor line: every part is prepped, coated and checked by the people doing the work.

Work We Finish for Stroud Customers

  • Parts and fabrications for mill-based workshops
  • Gates, railings and garden furniture
  • Farm and smallholding equipment from the Cotswold edge
  • Vintage machinery and restoration parts
  • Wheels and vehicle parts

Why Choose MB Finishings in Stroud?

  • Everything under one roof. Shot blasting, bead blasting, vapour blasting, laser cleaning, powder coating and Cerakote, all done in-house.
  • Coated by hand. Our powder coating is fully offline, so every part gets proper attention rather than a conveyor line.
  • Checked by eye. Every job is quality controlled before it leaves the unit.
  • Local family business. Eleven years of experience, run by the people doing the work.
